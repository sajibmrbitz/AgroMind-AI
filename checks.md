## 📋 Section A: Congress Submission Form Copy-Paste Dossier

### 1. Executive Summary & Abstract
AgroMind AI is a farmer-centric agricultural price intelligence and decision-support system tailored for Bangladesh. Built on validated retail price data from the Mendeley AgriPriceBD dataset (covering essential staples: Garlic, Chickpea, Green Chilli, Cucumber, and Sweet Pumpkin), the platform directly addresses extreme rural market volatility. It combines time-series econometric modeling (ARIMA with 80% confidence intervals benchmarked against persistence baselines) with calendar-month seasonality decomposition and context-aware, cautious Bengali advice generated by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash. To guarantee reliability in remote rural areas with intermittent connectivity or disabled API keys, AgroMind AI features an offline deterministic Bengali advisory engine.

### 2. Core Problem & Socio-Economic Impact
- **Extreme Price Fluctuations:** Perishable vegetables like green chilli and cucumber fluctuate by up to 300% across seasonal transitions, flooding, and festive peaks, often forcing distress selling by smallholders.
- **Information Asymmetry:** Middlemen (arotdars/farias) exploit rural farmers' lack of historical price benchmarks and upcoming market expectations.
- **Low Digital & Language Literacy:** Existing agricultural tools are in complex English or rely on opaque deep learning models without confidence intervals or local language explanations. AgroMind AI delivers pure Bengali (বাংলা) explanations, clear BDT/kg metrics, and transparent historical comparisons.
- **SDG Alignment:** Directly advances UN Sustainable Development Goals: SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth), and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

### 3. System Architecture & Modularity
- **Frontend Layer (`app.py`):** Lightweight, responsive Streamlit dashboard supporting desktop and mobile smartphone field views, bilingual (বাংলা / English) language switcher, first-landing commodity selection pop-up dialog (`@st.dialog`), interactive price visualizations with 80% confidence corridor bands, and one-click CSV data export for offline farm planning.
- **Forecasting Engine (`backend/forecast.py`):** Data cleaning (treating portal reporting zeros as missing, daily reindexing, forward/backward filling), ARIMA(1,1,1) with 80% confidence bands, Naive persistence baseline, input validation, and walk-forward cross-validation (MAE comparison).
- **Market Dynamics & Metadata (`backend/market.py`):** Crop seasonality profiles, Bengali vernacular names, typical sowing/harvesting calendars (রবি/খরিফ), volatility classifications, key cultivation districts, storage advisories, and structured data export utilities.
- **Hybrid Intelligence Layer (`backend/llm.py`):** 
  - Dual-mode architecture: Google Gemini 2.0 Flash for natural Bengali advisory + Deterministic statistical rule-based offline fallback.
  - Safe API communication: Header-based authentication (`x-goog-api-key`), exponential backoff retry for transient 429/503 errors, bounded timeouts.
  - Ethical Guardrails: No false certainty, no financial guarantees, clear historical disclaimers, zero transmission of private user data or raw CSV rows.

### 5. Responsible AI, Privacy & Security Guardrails
- **Header-Based Authentication:** API keys are never appended as URL query parameters (`?key=...`); instead, they are transmitted strictly in HTTP headers (`x-goog-api-key`), protecting credentials from browser histories, proxy logs, and error tracebacks.
- **Data Minimization:** No personally identifiable information (PII) or raw dataset rows are sent to external APIs. Only aggregate summary statistics (overall mean, peak month, trend percentage) are transmitted.
- **Retail vs. Farmgate Price Gap:** Prominently alerts farmers that data reflects urban retail mid-prices, and that farmgate prices are typically 15–30% lower due to transportation and intermediary costs.
- **Unpredictable Shocks Disclaimer:** Enforces statistical humility by stating that sudden floods, droughts, or border tariff changes cannot be predicted from historical series alone.

### 6. Deployment & Reproducibility Specifications
- **Containerization:** Production Dockerfile with multi-stage caching, non-root user conventions, and healthcheck endpoints (`/_stcore/health`).
- **Dependencies:** Constrained in `requirements.txt` (numpy, pandas, statsmodels, streamlit).
- **Environment:** Safe `.env.example` template provided.
